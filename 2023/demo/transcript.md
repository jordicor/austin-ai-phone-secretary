# Demo transcripts (July 2023)

Two recordings of the same call, made while testing Austin in July 2023. The call is in Spanish; the English translation is mine. Timestamps are approximate. The pauses between turns (two to five seconds) are the real latency of the 2023 pipeline: speech-to-text, GPT-4 and text-to-speech, one after another, over a phone line.

Both human voices are mine: I called Austin pretending to be "Pepe" from Endesa, the electricity company, and then took Austin's call on my own phone (an OpenPhone number).
